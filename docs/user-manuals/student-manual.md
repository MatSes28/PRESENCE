## Attendance Tracking

### How Attendance Works

#### RFID Card System

1. **Get Your Card**: Collect RFID card from registrar
2. **Register Card**: Link card to your account in profile
3. **Check In**: Tap card at classroom entrance
4. **Check Out**: Optional exit tap (for duration tracking)

#### Sensor Validation

- **Entry Sensors**: Detect arrival at classroom
- **Exit Sensors**: Track departure time
- **Validation**: System confirms legitimate attendance

#### Manual Entry

- **Faculty Override**: Instructor can mark attendance
- **Administrator Action**: For system issues
- **Special Cases**: Medical or other approved absences

### Daily Check-in Process

#### Morning Classes

1. **Arrive Early**: Be at classroom 5-10 minutes before start
2. **Tap Card**: Use RFID reader at entrance
3. **Verify**: Check dashboard for confirmation
4. **Enter Class**: Proceed to your seat

#### Lab Sessions

1. **Check Schedule**: Confirm lab location and time
2. **Arrive On Time**: Labs require punctuality
3. **Tap Card**: Check in at lab entrance
4. **Get Assignment**: Note your computer assignment
5. **Begin Work**: Start lab activities

### Attendance Status Types

#### Status Indicators

- 🟢 **Present**: Successfully checked in on time
- 🟡 **Late**: Arrived after class start (within grace period)
- 🔴 **Absent**: Did not check in or marked absent
- 🔵 **Excused**: Approved absence (medical, official business)

#### Grace Periods

- **Regular Classes**: 15-minute grace period
- **Lab Sessions**: 5-minute grace period
- **Special Cases**: As determined by faculty

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### RFID Card Problems

**Issue**: Card not recognized
**Solutions**:

- Ensure card is registered to your account
- Check card validity with registrar
- Clean card surface
- Try different reader

**Issue**: Multiple check-ins
**Solutions**:

- Wait 30 seconds between taps
- Check if card is damaged
- Contact IT support
- Use manual entry request

#### App Issues

**Issue**: App not working
**Solutions**:

- Check internet connection
- Restart app
- Update to latest version
- Clear app cache

**Issue**: Notifications not received
**Solutions**:

- Check notification settings
- Verify email address
- Enable push notifications
- Check spam folder

#### Login Problems

**Issue**: Can't access account
**Solutions**:

- Reset password
- Check email spelling
- Contact registrar for account issues
- Try different browser/device

## Academic Policies

### Attendance Requirements

#### BSIT Program Standards

- **Minimum Attendance**: 80% per subject
- **Excused Absences**: Medical certificates required
- **Late Policy**: More than 3 lates = 1 absence
- **Consequences**: Below 80% may affect grades

#### Subject-Specific Rules

- **Laboratory Courses**: 100% attendance required
- **Major Subjects**: Strict attendance monitoring
- **Electives**: More flexible policies
- **Review Classes**: Mandatory attendance

### Absence Procedures

#### Planned Absences

1. **Inform Faculty**: Email instructor in advance
2. **Provide Documentation**: Medical certificate or letter
3. **Make Up Work**: Complete missed assignments
4. **Record Absence**: System will mark as excused

#### Emergency Absences

1. **Contact Faculty**: As soon as possible
2. **Submit Documentation**: Within 3 days
3. **Arrange Make-up**: Schedule missed work
4. **Monitor Attendance**: Track impact on grades

### Academic Integrity

#### Attendance Ethics

- **Personal Responsibility**: Your attendance record
- **No Proxy Attendance**: Cannot use others' cards
- **System Integrity**: Report technical issues
- **Academic Honesty**: Accurate attendance reporting

#### Consequences of Violations

- **Warning**: First offense notification
- **Grade Penalty**: Attendance-based deductions
- **Academic Probation**: Repeated violations
- **Disciplinary Action**: Serious infractions
